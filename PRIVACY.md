# Celtaury Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 11, 2026

At Celtaury, we believe your data belongs to you. That is all.

## What Data We Collect
**None.** 

Cura and all Celtaury applications are built with a strict privacy-first architecture. 
- We do not collect personal data.
- We do not use analytics or crash reporting SDKs.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not transmit any files or storage data from your device to external servers.

All processing happens 100% locally on your device.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us via our GitHub repository.
